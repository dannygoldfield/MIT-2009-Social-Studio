# MIT sound sources for Test Studio

Research checked September 17, 2026. These are leads for the audio generator; no new online audio has been imported and nobody has been contacted.

## Resonating MIT — campus recordings and sound map

A 2014 MIT/Berklee project documented campus sound installations and let people pin recordings to an interactive map. Examples include the Stata Center metal staircase, Walker Memorial, and ambient sound in Lobby 7. This is the closest match to an archive of MIT campus sounds.

- [MIT project article](https://arts.mit.edu/resonating-mit-scanner-vitiello/)
- [Original map](http://resonatingmit.xvm.mit.edu/) — did not load during this research; raw files and reuse terms remain unverified.
- [ACT Project Archivist Thera Webb](https://act.mit.edu/about/people/thera-webb/) — a possible route to locating project records and the people who hold the recordings.

Possible fit: campus atmosphere for Sound beds; unusual resonances for Wildcards. Confirm access and permission before incorporating recordings.

## Greatest Hits — recorded object impacts and scrapes

MIT researchers recorded objects being hit and scratched with a drumstick. The sample gallery contains actual recordings, distinct from the research project's synthesized examples. MIT authorship is documented; the recordings' physical location on campus is not established.

- [Project, downloads, and dataset license](https://andrewowens.com/vis/)
- [Recorded sample gallery](https://www.andrewowens.com/vis/hits.html)
- [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The project lists full-resolution and smaller video bundles (50 GB and 20 GB). Bundle downloads have not been tested. The dataset license permits reuse and adaptation with attribution, a license link, and an indication of changes.

Best initial fit: Sound Effects, with wood taps, metal impacts, ceramic clinks, and scrapes; selected sounds could also become rhythmic Wildcards. Start with a small audition set rather than importing the entire research dataset.

## LOUD — laboratory clicks and chirps recorded at CSAIL

The source page explicitly identifies recordings from MIT CSAIL's 1,020-node microphone array. Calibration experiments used a loudspeaker click followed by frequency-sweep chirps. The page links a source sound and large experimental data files.

- [LOUD data and sound download page](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/cag/mic-array/data.html)

Possible fit: laboratory-flavored Wildcards. A reuse license was not found on this page; public download access alone does not establish reuse permission. Files have not been imported or auditioned.

## Suggested next step

Audition a small Greatest Hits selection for immediate practical use, while separately tracing the Resonating MIT archive for authentic campus recordings. Keep creator, source URL, license, and edit details with any imported asset so attribution is easy when publishing.
